Content Opportunity Scoring for Search Review Prioritization

FlyRank ML Internship — Research Paper

Author: Fatima Iqbal

Abstract

This study asks which observable search-performance signals can be combined into a repeatable score for prioritizing content pages for refresh or review.

1. Introduction / Problem Statement

Content teams may have many pages that could potentially be reviewed, but limited time to inspect every page. This project investigates whether observable search-performance signals can help prioritize which pages should receive human review first.

2. Data

This study uses the FlyRank ML Internship dataset and focuses on observable search, traffic, engagement, and content-performance signals.

3. Methodology

A Random Forest classifier was evaluated against a majority-class baseline using client-grouped validation and leakage checks.

4. Results

Majority baseline: 54.21%

Random Forest: 57.24%

5. Limitations & Honest Framing

The results are directional decision-support evidence. They do not predict Google's ranking algorithm and do not establish causal effects of content refreshes.

6. Ranked Recommendations

  1. Review declining pages.
  2. Review weak-engagement pages.
  3. Review high-demand pages with weak visibility.
  4. Review aging content.
  5. Monitor mixed-signal pages.

The analysis and capstone notebook are available in the public GitHub repository.
